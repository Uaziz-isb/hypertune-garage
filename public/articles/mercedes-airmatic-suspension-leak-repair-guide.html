Why Does Your Mercedes-Benz Sag Overnight?

If your Mercedes-Benz S-Class, E-Class, or GLE/ML sags to one corner after parking overnight, the Airmatic air suspension has developed a pneumatic pressure leak.

Ignoring a sagging strut forces the pneumatic compressor to run continuously while driving, rapidly burning out the compressor motor and melting relay contacts.

Common Symptoms of Airmatic Failure

  • Cluster displays: "Stop, Car Too Low" or "Airmatic: Visit Workshop".
  • Hissing sound audible near wheel arches after turning off the engine.
  • Harsh or bouncy ride quality over uneven road surfaces.
  • Suspension compressor pump buzzing loudly or running non-stop.
